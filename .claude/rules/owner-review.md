# How the owner reviews numbered proposals

When the owner answers a list of numbered proposals or review items, he
writes only about the items he has something to say about. Every item he
does not mention is accepted.

Number every proposal and give a recommendation for each. Record the items
he did not mention as accepted in the document; do not ask about them
again. Ask only about the items he commented on, and only when his comment
leaves a real choice open.
